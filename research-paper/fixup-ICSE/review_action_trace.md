## Notes on unresolved review requests

- The remaining unaddressed items are not blocked by missing GPU hardware.
- They instead require new work outside the faithful rewrite scope: repeated LLM queries, stronger compiler-grade or externally trained baselines beyond the lightweight grouped-CV references added here, and human validation of the feature labels.
- The raw feature-prevalence tables and multiple-comparison-corrected exploratory summaries are now recovered from the restored PostgreSQL dump files and exported in `generated/feature_vote_feature_summary.csv` and `generated/feature_vote_model_prompt_stats.csv`.
